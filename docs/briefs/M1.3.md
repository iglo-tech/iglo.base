Goal: load, validate, encrypt and store the app settings in `_params` exactly as upstream v0.41.0 does, reload them at startup and after saves, enforce the superuser IP whitelist that reads them, and serve `GET /api/settings` and `PATCH /api/settings` with upstream's bodies, errors and masking.

  - All settings scenarios from apis/settings_test.go and the superuser-IP scenarios pass against both servers in the differential suite (`bun run test:reference`) and against iglo.base alone in `bun test`, on copies of the vendored fixture, except those marked `pending` M4.4 (email test), M5.4 (S3 test) or M4.6 (Apple secret), each failing for its stated reason.

  - Branch: task/M1.3-settings. PR title: "M1.3: Settings model and Settings API".

  - There is no time limit; finish the task. Stop early only for a real blocker (a design conflict the lead must decide, or a usage/rate-limit error), and describe it in the PR and your reply.
